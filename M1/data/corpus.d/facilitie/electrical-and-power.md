# Electrical and power supply

Lights, fans, switches, sockets, corridor lighting and power supply in all buildings are maintained by the facilities electrical team.

Report a dead socket, flickering tube light, tripped circuit, sparking point or exposed wiring on the facilities portal with the room or corridor location — sparking or burning smells are emergencies; call the hotline and keep people away from the point.

Power backup (generator/inverter) covers the admin block, labs and the library during outages; classroom blocks may not be on backup.

Scheduled power maintenance is announced on the facilities notice a day in advance with the affected blocks and timings.
