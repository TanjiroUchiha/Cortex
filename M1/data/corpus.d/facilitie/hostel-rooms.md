# Hostel rooms and repairs

Hostel room allocation, room changes and in-room repairs are handled by the facilities hostel desk in the admin block.

Room repairs cover furniture (cot, table, chair, cupboard), lights, fans, switches, windows, doors and latches. Raise a repair request on the facilities portal with your hostel block, room number and a description; non-urgent repairs are scheduled within three working days. Electrical faults, water leaks and broken door locks count as urgent and are handled same-day.

Room change requests open at the start of each semester and are processed by the hostel desk against the published vacancy list. Inter-hostel transfers need warden approval.

Linen, mattress and pest-control complaints go through the same portal under the housekeeping category.
