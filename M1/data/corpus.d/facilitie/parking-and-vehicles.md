# Parking and vehicle passes

Student and staff parking zones are marked on the campus map — two-wheelers at the gates, cars in the designated lots, and hostel parking behind the hostel blocks.

A parking sticker (vehicle pass) is required for all vehicles on campus; apply on the facilities portal with the registration number and your ID. Temporary visitor passes are issued at Gate 1 security.

Report damaged parking barriers, blocked slots or vehicles parked in no-parking zones to the facilities hotline — wrong parking is clamped and released at the security office.

Cycle stands and EV charging points are maintained by facilities; a faulty charging point can be reported on the portal under electrical faults.
