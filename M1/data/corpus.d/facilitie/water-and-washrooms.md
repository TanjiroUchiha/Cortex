# Water supply and washrooms

Drinking water coolers, water purifiers, washrooms and plumbing in all buildings are maintained by the facilities plumbing crew.

Report no water supply, leaking taps, blocked drains, broken flush systems or unclean washrooms through the facilities portal with the building, floor and nearest room number. Water coolers that are not dispensing cold water or have a damaged tap should be reported the same way.

Washrooms are cleaned twice daily on working days. If a washroom needs immediate attention (overflow, no water, broken lock), call the facilities hotline rather than the portal.

Drinking water quality is tested monthly; the test reports are filed with the facilities office and can be requested through the portal.
