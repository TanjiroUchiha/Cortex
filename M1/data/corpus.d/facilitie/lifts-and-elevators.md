# Lifts and elevators

Lifts (elevators) in all academic blocks, hostels and the admin building are maintained by the facilities team through an annual maintenance contract.

If a lift is not working, stuck between floors, making unusual noise, or its doors are not closing properly, report it through the facilities portal with the building name and lift number, or call the facilities hotline for urgent cases. Every lift car has the hotline number and the lift ID printed inside.

If someone is trapped inside a lift, call the facilities emergency line immediately and stay on the phone — do not attempt to force the doors open. A technician is dispatched first and the maintenance contractor is called when required.

Routine preventive maintenance runs monthly, usually early morning before classes. A notice is placed at the lift lobby the day before each scheduled shutdown.
