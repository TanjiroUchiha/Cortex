# Campus contacts and directions

The main help desk sits in the admin block lobby and is open 9am to 5pm on working days — it redirects visitors and answers campus questions of any kind.

Department-wise contacts, staff directory, extension numbers and office room numbers are listed on the staff directory page of the portal. Emergency numbers (security control room, medical room, facilities hotline, IT help desk) are printed on the back of the ID card.

The campus map with building names, gates and parking zones is on the portal's campus info section; visitor passes are issued at Gate 1 security.

Lost and found items are deposited at the security control room near Gate 1 — carry your ID to claim an item.
