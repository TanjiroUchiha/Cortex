# Academic calendar and holidays

Term start and end dates, examination windows, fee deadlines, holidays and semester breaks are published in the academic calendar on the portal before each term begins.

Any change to the calendar (shifted exam windows, additional holidays, term extensions) is notified through the portal announcement banner and circulars — individual departments do not publish separate calendars.

The calendar shows institute holidays only; departmental events and make-up classes are scheduled by each department and do not appear on it.

Calendar downloads are available in PDF and as a subscribable feed for calendar apps.
