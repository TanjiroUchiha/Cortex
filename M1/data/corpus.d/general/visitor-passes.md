# Visitor passes and gate entry

All visitors enter through Gate 1 with a visitor pass issued by security — the host student or staff member must confirm the visit.

Parents and guardians visiting for administrative work are issued day passes; overnight stay near hostels is not permitted without prior approval from student affairs.

Entry for delivery agents, cab drivers and vendors stops at the gate — pickups happen at the designated delivery point near Gate 1.

Official guests and interview candidates are issued gate passes through the hosting department's office.
