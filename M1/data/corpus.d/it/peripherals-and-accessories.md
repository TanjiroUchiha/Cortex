# Peripherals and accessories

Keyboards, mice, chargers, adapters, HDMI cables and monitor arms for institute machines are stocked by the IT stores counter.

Request a replacement or a new peripheral on the IT portal under hardware accessories — replacements for institute equipment are free, and staff can borrow presentation clickers and adapters for a day.

A faulty charger or cable on an institute laptop should be exchanged at the counter, not repaired. Personal device accessories are not supplied.

Projector remotes and laser pointers for classrooms are issued by IT stores against the room booking reference.
