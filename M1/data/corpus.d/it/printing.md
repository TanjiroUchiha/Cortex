# Printing

Shared printers in study areas, labs and admin areas are managed by IT, including toner, paper jams and print queues.

Print from any lab machine or through the print portal upload page using your institute account. Color printing is only available on the library printer and is charged to your printing balance.

If a printer is jammed, out of paper, printing faded pages or refusing jobs, report it on the IT help portal with the printer ID shown on its front label — do not pull stuck paper yourself. Printer hardware faults go to IT, but a physically broken printer cabinet or a tripped power socket goes to facilities.

Refunds for failed prints are credited automatically within a day; if a charge is not reversed, open a ticket with the job ID from your print history.
