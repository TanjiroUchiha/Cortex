# IT help desk and tickets

The IT help desk is the single point of contact for accounts, WiFi, email, software, printing and institute-owned hardware.

Raise a ticket on the IT help portal — every ticket gets an ID and a queue position. Walk-in support is available at the help desk in the admin block, 9am to 5.30pm on working days, for password resets and urgent account problems.

Ticket priorities: account lockouts and suspected compromise are same-day; broken institute hardware is next working day; software installs and quota changes are scheduled within two working days.

Check ticket status on the portal — replies go to your institute email automatically.
