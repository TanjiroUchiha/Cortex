# Passwords and accounts

Institute accounts cover the portal, WiFi, email and lab machines — one set of credentials for all of them.

To reset a forgotten password, open the IT help portal and choose the password reset option; a reset link is sent to your registered recovery email and expires after 30 minutes. If your recovery email is inaccessible, visit the IT help desk in the admin block with your ID card.

Accounts lock after five failed sign-in attempts and unlock automatically after 15 minutes. If your account is locked repeatedly, or you suspect someone else is using it, report it to the IT help desk immediately — suspected account misuse is investigated by IT security.

New student accounts are created automatically at admission. New staff accounts are created on HR joining confirmation.
