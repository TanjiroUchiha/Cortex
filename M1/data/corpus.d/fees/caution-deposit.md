# Caution deposit

The caution deposit collected at admission is refundable at the end of the programme or on withdrawal, after all dues are cleared.

Claim it on the fees portal under deposits with the no-dues clearance from the library, labs, hostel and sports sections. Refund is processed to the registered bank account within ten working days of clearance.

Deductions against the deposit — unpaid fines, damaged institute property, pending dues — are itemised on the refund slip; disputes go to the fees office within thirty days of the slip date.

The deposit does not earn interest and cannot be adjusted against regular semester fees while you are enrolled.
