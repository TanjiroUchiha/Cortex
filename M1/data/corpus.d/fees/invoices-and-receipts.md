# Invoices and receipts

An itemized invoice and a payment receipt are generated for every transaction on the fees portal and can be downloaded any time from the receipts section.

Receipts carry the transaction ID, payment head (tuition, hostel, mess, examination), and payment mode. Employers or sponsors who need an official invoice for reimbursement should use the downloadable invoice — it carries the institute seal digitally.

If a receipt shows a wrong name, roll number or payment head, raise a correction request on the fees portal with the transaction ID; corrected receipts are reissued within three working days.

For payments made at the fees counter by cash or card, the printed receipt is the only proof — collect it before leaving the counter.
