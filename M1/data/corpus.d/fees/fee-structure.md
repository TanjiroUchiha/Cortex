# Fee structure

The official fee structure for every programme — tuition, examination, hostel, mess, transport and one-time heads — is published on the fees portal under programme fee structure.

The structure is fixed at admission for the duration of the programme; revisions apply only to new batches. Your personal payable amount may differ after scholarships, waivers or lateral-entry adjustments.

A signed fee structure letter for bank loans or sponsors is generated from the portal and carries the registrar's digital seal.

If the portal shows an unexpected head or amount, raise a fees ticket with your programme and admission year — corrections are verified against the admission records.
