# Fee payment deadlines

Semester tuition, hostel and mess fees must be paid by the dates published on the academic calendar. The fees portal shows your exact dues and the deadline for each head.

Late payments attract a fine per the fee circular — the amount depends on how many weeks past the deadline the payment is made. Exam registration is blocked until dues are cleared.

An approved installment plan, where available, must be arranged with the fees office before the deadline — requests after the deadline are not considered. Approved installments still appear as pending dues on the portal until each installment is paid.

Online payments that fail after money is debited are reconciled automatically within seven working days; keep the bank transaction reference for the fees helpdesk if it does not resolve.
