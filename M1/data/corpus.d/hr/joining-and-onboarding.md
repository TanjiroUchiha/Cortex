# Joining and onboarding

New staff complete joining formalities at the HR desk in the admin block on their first working day — carry the offer letter, photo ID, education certificates, bank details and two passport photos.

The joining kit includes the employee handbook, ID card application form and the portal account activation steps. Biometric attendance registration happens at the HR desk the same day.

Probation details, notice period and the reporting manager are listed in the offer letter; the onboarding checklist on the HR portal tracks pending document submissions.

Any mismatch in name or personal details on institute records must be corrected at the HR desk within the first week — later corrections need an affidavit.
