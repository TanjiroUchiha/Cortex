# Grievances and escalation

Workplace grievances — conflicts with a manager, unfair assessment, harassment or policy concerns — are raised confidentially on the HR portal under grievances.

Every grievance gets a case ID and an assigned HR business partner. The first response is within three working days; sensitive cases are escalated to the grievance committee with the reporter's consent.

For harassment complaints there is also a direct confidential channel to the internal committee listed on the HR portal — bypassing the regular queue is allowed for these cases.

Grievance outcomes and any policy actions are communicated on the portal; nothing is shared with the reporting manager without the complainant's knowledge.
