# Emergency shower and eyewash check

## Purpose and scope
This record applies to approved lab users and the equipment or space listed in the local risk assessment. The assigned technician records inspection date, flow test and defect; blocked equipment is reported as an immediate safety issue.

## Responsibilities
The requester supplies complete, accurate information. Laboratory Operations verifies the record, records its decision and communicates any required next step through the approved portal.

## Process and records
The assigned technician records inspection date, flow test and defect; blocked equipment is reported as an immediate safety issue. The responsible owner is Laboratory Operations. Submit a case through the Laboratory Booking Portal with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.
Keep only the records required for the case. Do not place identity documents, medical details, credentials or payment secrets in a general-purpose ticket.

## Exceptions and escalation
An exception must be approved by the named record owner and recorded with its reason and expiry. If the issue creates immediate risk, contact Campus Security; routine questions should remain with the responsible service desk.
