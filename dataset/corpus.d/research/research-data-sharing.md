# Research data sharing approval

## Purpose and scope
This record applies to institution-sponsored research; sponsor terms and approved ethics determinations remain controlling. A sharing request names the recipient, purpose, data fields and legal basis; de-identification alone does not remove all re-identification risk.

## Responsibilities
The requester supplies complete, accurate information. Research Office verifies the record, records its decision and communicates any required next step through the approved portal.

## Process and records
A sharing request names the recipient, purpose, data fields and legal basis; de-identification alone does not remove all re-identification risk. The responsible owner is Research Office. Submit a case through the Research Services Portal with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.
Keep only the records required for the case. Do not place identity documents, medical details, credentials or payment secrets in a general-purpose ticket.

## Exceptions and escalation
An exception must be approved by the named record owner and recorded with its reason and expiry. If the issue creates immediate risk, contact Campus Security; routine questions should remain with the responsible service desk.

## Review
The department owner reviews this guidance when the governing schedule, service workflow or institutional approval changes. A newer active version takes precedence over a superseded copy.

## Audit trail
The owner records the decision date, approving role and any condition attached to the decision. Access to the case is limited to the roles listed in the metadata.
