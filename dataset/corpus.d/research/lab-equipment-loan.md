# Portable lab equipment loan

## Summary
**What should the reader know?** The borrower records asset tag, calibration state and return date; equipment remains within approved locations unless the lab manager authorizes transit.

## Action
Use the Laboratory Booking Portal for a case-specific answer. The service desk at labs@cdu.example can route a request but cannot approve an exception outside its delegated role.

This record applies to approved lab users and the equipment or space listed in the local risk assessment.
