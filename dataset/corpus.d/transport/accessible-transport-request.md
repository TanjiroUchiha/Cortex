# Accessible transport accommodation

## Summary
**What should the reader know?** Students or employees request an accessible vehicle through Student Services or HR; route and boarding details are confirmed privately.

## Action
Use the Transport Portal for a case-specific answer. The service desk at transport@cdu.example can route a request but cannot approve an exception outside its delegated role.

This record applies to registered campus riders and scheduled institutional transport.
