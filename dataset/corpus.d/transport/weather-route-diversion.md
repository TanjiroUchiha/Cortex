# Weather-related route diversion

## Purpose and scope
This record applies to registered campus riders and scheduled institutional transport. Transport posts affected stops and safe alternatives when weather closes a road; riders must not wait in an unsafe roadside location.

## Responsibilities
The requester supplies complete, accurate information. Campus Transport verifies the record, records its decision and communicates any required next step through the approved portal.

## Process and records
Transport posts affected stops and safe alternatives when weather closes a road; riders must not wait in an unsafe roadside location. The responsible owner is Campus Transport. Submit a case through the Transport Portal with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.
Keep only the records required for the case. Do not place identity documents, medical details, credentials or payment secrets in a general-purpose ticket.

## Exceptions and escalation
An exception must be approved by the named record owner and recorded with its reason and expiry. If the issue creates immediate risk, contact Campus Security; routine questions should remain with the responsible service desk.
