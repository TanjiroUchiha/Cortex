# Course withdrawal

## Purpose and scope
This record applies to enrolled students and the staff who maintain the official academic record. A withdrawal request after add/drop requires the advisor and program chair; the transcript records a W and tuition adjustments follow the published fee schedule.

## Responsibilities
The requester supplies complete, accurate information. Academic Affairs verifies the record, records its decision and communicates any required next step through the approved portal.

## Process and records
A withdrawal request after add/drop requires the advisor and program chair; the transcript records a W and tuition adjustments follow the published fee schedule. The responsible owner is Academic Affairs. Submit a case through the Academic Records Portal with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.
Keep only the records required for the case. Do not place identity documents, medical details, credentials or payment secrets in a general-purpose ticket.

## Exceptions and escalation
An exception must be approved by the named record owner and recorded with its reason and expiry. If the issue creates immediate risk, contact Campus Security; routine questions should remain with the responsible service desk.

## Review
The department owner reviews this guidance when the governing schedule, service workflow or institutional approval changes. A newer active version takes precedence over a superseded copy.

## Example
If a request related to course withdrawal is incomplete, the service team returns it with the missing item and keeps the original case reference. The requester adds the evidence to that case rather than opening a second record.
