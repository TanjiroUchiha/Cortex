# Laboratory incident and near-miss report

## Steps
1. Confirm that the request concerns laboratory incident and near-miss report and identify the responsible unit.
2. Submit the relevant record identifier and supporting evidence through the Laboratory Booking Portal.
3. Save the case reference and check the portal for a decision or a request for clarification.
4. If the record remains unresolved after the stated service window, contact labs@cdu.example with the existing reference.

## Rule
Report injuries, spills and near misses promptly with location and material involved; preserve the scene when safe and do not assign blame in the initial report. The responsible owner is Laboratory Operations. Submit a case through the Laboratory Booking Portal with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.

## Exception
Do not create a duplicate request while an existing case is under review; urgent safety or privacy risks use the designated emergency channel.
