# Industrial training completion

## Steps
1. Confirm that the request concerns industrial training completion and identify the responsible unit.
2. Submit the relevant record identifier and supporting evidence through the Academic Records Portal.
3. Save the case reference and check the portal for a decision or a request for clarification.
4. If the record remains unresolved after the stated service window, contact academics@cdu.example with the existing reference.

## Rule
The training office accepts a signed placement record, attendance log and reflective report; incomplete evidence is returned for correction before credit is posted. The responsible owner is Academic Affairs. Submit a case through the Academic Records Portal with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.

## Exception
Do not create a duplicate request while an existing case is under review; urgent safety or privacy risks use the designated emergency channel.
