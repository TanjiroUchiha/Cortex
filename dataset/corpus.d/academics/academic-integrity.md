# Academic integrity and collaboration

## Summary
**What should the reader know?** Students may discuss concepts but submit their own analysis unless a task explicitly permits group work; sources, tools and reused material must be acknowledged.

## Action
Use the Academic Records Portal for a case-specific answer. The service desk at academics@cdu.example can route a request but cannot approve an exception outside its delegated role.

This record applies to enrolled students and the staff who maintain the official academic record.
