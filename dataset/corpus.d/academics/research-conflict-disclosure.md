# Research conflict-of-interest disclosure

## Summary
**What should the reader know?** Investigators update financial or personal-interest declarations at proposal, award and material change points; management plans are documented.

## Action
Use the Research Services Portal for a case-specific answer. The service desk at research@cdu.example can route a request but cannot approve an exception outside its delegated role.

This record applies to institution-sponsored research; sponsor terms and approved ethics determinations remain controlling.
