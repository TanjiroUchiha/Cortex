# Research award amendment request

## Steps
1. Confirm that the request concerns research award amendment request and identify the responsible unit.
2. Submit the relevant record identifier and supporting evidence through the Research Services Portal.
3. Save the case reference and check the portal for a decision or a request for clarification.
4. If the record remains unresolved after the stated service window, contact research@cdu.example with the existing reference.

## Rule
An amendment states the change, sponsor clause and budget impact; the original award remains controlling until signed approval is filed. The responsible owner is Research Office. Submit a case through the Research Services Portal with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.

## Exception
Do not create a duplicate request while an existing case is under review; urgent safety or privacy risks use the designated emergency channel.
