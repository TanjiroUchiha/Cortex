# Leave policy

Annual leave (earned leave) must be applied for in the HR self-service portal and approved by the reporting manager before the leave date. Leave taken without approval is marked as loss of pay.

Sick leave does not need prior approval but requires a medical certificate uploaded to the portal after three consecutive days of absence. Shorter sick leave needs no certificate.

Casual leave is capped at twelve days a year, cannot be combined with annual leave around it, and is granted for a maximum of three consecutive days.

Leave balances reset every January and unused annual leave carries forward up to ten days. The leave calendar and your current balance are shown on the portal dashboard.

Annual leave requests must be approved by the reporting manager before the planned leave date. Sick leave requires a certificate after three consecutive days.
