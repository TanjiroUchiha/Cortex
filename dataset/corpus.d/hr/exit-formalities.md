# Exit Formalities

Resignation, notice period and relieving letters

Staff resignation goes through the HR portal under separations — the notice period in the appointment letter applies from the date of submission, and the last working day is confirmed in writing by HR after the manager's acceptance.

Before the final day, the exit checklist covers asset return (laptop, ID card, keys), pending dues, and the knowledge handover; the relieving letter and experience certificate are issued only after every row is cleared. Pending salary and leave encashment settle in the next payroll cycle.

For notice-period waivers, checklist status or certificate timelines, contact the HR Service Centre — hr@campus.edu (ext 4410).
