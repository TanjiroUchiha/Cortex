# Work from home

Work-from-home is allowed up to two days a month for eligible roles, applied for on the HR portal and approved by the reporting manager before the date.

WFH days count as working days and require punch-in through the portal's remote attendance option — a missing remote punch is treated like a missed punch and needs regularization.

Roles that need on-campus presence (labs, workshops, front desks, examinations duty) are not eligible; the eligibility list is maintained by HR.

Extended WFH for medical reasons needs a medical certificate and HR head approval and is decided case by case.
