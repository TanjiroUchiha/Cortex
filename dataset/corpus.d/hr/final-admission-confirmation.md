# Final admission confirmation

## Steps
1. Confirm that the request concerns final admission confirmation and identify the responsible unit.
2. Submit the relevant record identifier and supporting evidence through the Admissions Portal.
3. Save the case reference and check the portal for a decision or a request for clarification.
4. If the record remains unresolved after the stated service window, contact admissions@cdu.example with the existing reference.

## Rule
Confirmation is issued after identity, qualification and payment checks pass; applicants should rely on the dated portal letter as the final status. The responsible owner is Admissions. Submit a case through the Admissions Portal with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.

## Exception
Do not create a duplicate request while an existing case is under review; urgent safety or privacy risks use the designated emergency channel.
