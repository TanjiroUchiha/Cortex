# Employee insurance claim support

## Steps
1. Confirm that the request concerns employee insurance claim support and identify the responsible unit.
2. Submit the relevant record identifier and supporting evidence through the HR Service Portal.
3. Save the case reference and check the portal for a decision or a request for clarification.
4. If the record remains unresolved after the stated service window, contact hr@cdu.example with the existing reference.

## Rule
HR explains claim routing and required institutional employment confirmation; medical details are sent only through the insurer's secure channel. The responsible owner is Human Resources. Submit a case through the HR Service Portal with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.

## Exception
Do not create a duplicate request while an existing case is under review; urgent safety or privacy risks use the designated emergency channel.
