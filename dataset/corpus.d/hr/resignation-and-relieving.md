# Resignation and relieving

Resignation is submitted on the HR portal under separations and routed to the reporting manager and department head for approval. The notice period in the offer letter applies from the date of submission.

After approval, the relieving checklist opens on the portal: return the ID card, laptop and any institute equipment, clear library dues and lab dues, and get the no-dues form signed.

The relieving letter and experience certificate are issued on the last working day once all clearances are complete. The final settlement — pending salary, leave encashment and any recoveries — is processed within 30 days.

An exit interview with HR is scheduled in the final week; completing it is part of the clearance process.
