# Attendance

Attendance is recorded through the biometric terminals and ID card punches at building entrances.

If a punch was missed or recorded wrongly, submit an attendance regularization request on the HR portal within five working days of the missed punch — the reporting manager approves it online. More than three regularizations in a month require the department head's counter-approval.

Holidays, duty leave for official events and work-from-home days are marked automatically from approved records. If a marked absence looks wrong, open an HR ticket before the monthly attendance lock date — attendance is locked on the second working day of the following month.

Students' biometric attendance for labs and workshops follows the same regularization process through their department coordinator.

Attendance corrections must be submitted to HR within five working days of the missed punch.
