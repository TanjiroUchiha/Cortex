# Undergraduate admission eligibility

## Purpose and scope
This record applies to applicants in the stated intake and does not create an admission offer. Applicants must meet the published qualification and subject prerequisites for the selected program; meeting minimum eligibility does not guarantee a seat.

## Responsibilities
The requester supplies complete, accurate information. Admissions verifies the record, records its decision and communicates any required next step through the approved portal.

## Process and records
Applicants must meet the published qualification and subject prerequisites for the selected program; meeting minimum eligibility does not guarantee a seat. The responsible owner is Admissions. Submit a case through the Admissions Portal with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.
Keep only the records required for the case. Do not place identity documents, medical details, credentials or payment secrets in a general-purpose ticket.

## Exceptions and escalation
An exception must be approved by the named record owner and recorded with its reason and expiry. If the issue creates immediate risk, contact Campus Security; routine questions should remain with the responsible service desk.

## Review
The department owner reviews this guidance when the governing schedule, service workflow or institutional approval changes. A newer active version takes precedence over a superseded copy.

## Example
If a request related to undergraduate admission eligibility is incomplete, the service team returns it with the missing item and keeps the original case reference. The requester adds the evidence to that case rather than opening a second record.

## Audit trail
The owner records the decision date, approving role and any condition attached to the decision. Access to the case is limited to the roles listed in the metadata.
