# Experience and verification letters

Experience certificates, employment verification letters, relieving letters and salary certificates are requested on the HR portal under letters and documents.

Standard letters are issued within five working days; letters on institute letterhead for visas, loans or higher studies need the purpose stated in the request and may take up to ten working days.

Internship and bonafide letters for students are issued by the academic office, not HR — route those requests through the general administration counter.

Third-party employment verification (banks, landlords, background-check agencies) is answered directly by HR after the employee consents on the portal.
