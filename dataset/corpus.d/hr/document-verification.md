# Admission document verification

## Purpose and scope
This record applies to applicants in the stated intake and does not create an admission offer. The admissions team compares uploaded records with originals at verification; discrepancies are placed on hold and must be corrected by the stated date.

## Responsibilities
The requester supplies complete, accurate information. Admissions verifies the record, records its decision and communicates any required next step through the approved portal.

## Process and records
The admissions team compares uploaded records with originals at verification; discrepancies are placed on hold and must be corrected by the stated date. The responsible owner is Admissions. Submit a case through the Admissions Portal with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.
Keep only the records required for the case. Do not place identity documents, medical details, credentials or payment secrets in a general-purpose ticket.

## Exceptions and escalation
An exception must be approved by the named record owner and recorded with its reason and expiry. If the issue creates immediate risk, contact Campus Security; routine questions should remain with the responsible service desk.

## Review
The department owner reviews this guidance when the governing schedule, service workflow or institutional approval changes. A newer active version takes precedence over a superseded copy.
