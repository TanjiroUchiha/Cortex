# Payroll and payslips

Salary is credited on the last working day of each month to the bank account registered with HR at joining.

Payslips are published to the HR self-service portal on the salary date and stay available for download — older payslips are under the archive section. For a corrected payslip or a salary discrepancy, raise an HR ticket with the month and the line item in question; corrections appear in the next cycle.

Form 16 and other tax documents are published annually on the portal. To change your bank account, PAN or tax declaration, submit the corresponding form on the portal with a cancelled cheque or passbook copy — bank changes take effect from the next payroll cycle.

Reimbursement claims (travel, medical, LTA) are filed separately under the claims section, not through payroll tickets.
