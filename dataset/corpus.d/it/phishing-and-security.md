# Phishing and account security

Suspicious emails asking for passwords, OTPs, gift cards or payment details are phishing — do not click links or reply.

Forward the message to the IT security mailbox (listed on the IT portal), then delete it. If you already entered your password on a linked page, change it immediately from the IT help portal and report the incident the same day.

IT never asks for your password, OTP or PIN by mail, phone or chat. Official IT mails come from the institute domain only and never link to external sign-in pages.

Enable the two-step sign-in option on the account security page; it is mandatory for staff and recommended for students.
