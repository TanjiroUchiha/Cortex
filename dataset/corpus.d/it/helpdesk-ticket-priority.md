# IT helpdesk ticket priority

## Purpose and scope
This record applies to institution-managed accounts and devices; system-owner approval is required for privileged access. Tickets state affected users, service, error and workaround; outages affecting many users are triaged above single-device inconvenience.

## Responsibilities
The requester supplies complete, accurate information. Information Technology verifies the record, records its decision and communicates any required next step through the approved portal.

## Process and records
Tickets state affected users, service, error and workaround; outages affecting many users are triaged above single-device inconvenience. The responsible owner is Information Technology. Submit a case through the IT Service Desk with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.
Keep only the records required for the case. Do not place identity documents, medical details, credentials or payment secrets in a general-purpose ticket.

## Exceptions and escalation
An exception must be approved by the named record owner and recorded with its reason and expiry. If the issue creates immediate risk, contact Campus Security; routine questions should remain with the responsible service desk.

## Review
The department owner reviews this guidance when the governing schedule, service workflow or institutional approval changes. A newer active version takes precedence over a superseded copy.

## Audit trail
The owner records the decision date, approving role and any condition attached to the decision. Access to the case is limited to the roles listed in the metadata.
