# Laptop and hardware issues

Institute-issued laptops, desktops and lab machines are repaired by the IT hardware team.

For a device that will not boot, overheats, has a broken screen, keyboard or hinge, or fails after a drop or spill, raise a hardware ticket on the IT help portal with the asset tag on the underside of the machine. Power off liquid-damaged devices immediately and do not charge them.

A loaner laptop can be issued for the repair period if the machine is institute-owned and the ticket is marked as blocking coursework — personal devices are not covered.

RAM, SSD and battery replacements on institute machines are free under warranty; accidental damage may be charged per the IT asset policy circulated at issue.
