# Events and announcements

Institute-wide events, guest lectures, cultural fests and registration calls are announced on the portal's announcement feed and the notice boards near the admin block.

Event registrations run through the portal events page; some events cap seats and close registration early. Department-level events are announced by the departments themselves and may not appear on the institute feed.

Students wanting to organize an event submit the event proposal form on the portal — approval goes through the faculty advisor and the student affairs office, and hall bookings then follow the normal facilities booking process.

Announcements older than one semester move to the archive section of the feed and remain searchable.
