# Clubs And Events

Student clubs and campus events

Recognised clubs are listed on the portal's student life page with their faculty coordinators and joining process — most enrol at the start of each semester at the clubs fair. Starting a new club needs ten interested students and a faculty sponsor, filed through student affairs.

Event permissions — venues, timings, speakers, budgets — are approved on the event request form at least one week ahead; late requests are usually declined. Funding follows the approved budget lines only.

Club registration, event approvals and the student activities calendar are handled by the Student Affairs office — helpdesk@campus.edu (ext 4000).
