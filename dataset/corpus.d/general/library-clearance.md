# Library clearance for graduation or exit

## Summary
**What should the reader know?** Clearance requires return or settlement of every charged item; the Library sends a status to Student Services after the account is resolved.

## Action
Use the Library Account Portal for a case-specific answer. The service desk at library@cdu.example can route a request but cannot approve an exception outside its delegated role.

This record applies to active library users and items tracked in the library account system.
