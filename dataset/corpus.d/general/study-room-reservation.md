# Study room reservation

## Purpose and scope
This record applies to active library users and items tracked in the library account system. Groups reserve a room by time slot and expected headcount; users release an unused booking so others can reserve it.

## Responsibilities
The requester supplies complete, accurate information. Library Services verifies the record, records its decision and communicates any required next step through the approved portal.

## Process and records
Groups reserve a room by time slot and expected headcount; users release an unused booking so others can reserve it. The responsible owner is Library Services. Submit a case through the Library Account Portal with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.
Keep only the records required for the case. Do not place identity documents, medical details, credentials or payment secrets in a general-purpose ticket.

## Exceptions and escalation
An exception must be approved by the named record owner and recorded with its reason and expiry. If the issue creates immediate risk, contact Campus Security; routine questions should remain with the responsible service desk.

The department owner reviews this guidance when the governing schedule, service workflow or institutional approval changes. A newer active version takes precedence over a superseded copy.
