# Clubs, councils and student chapters

Technical clubs, cultural societies and student chapters are registered with the student affairs office — the active list with coordinators is on the portal's campus life page.

Joining a club usually opens during the first two weeks of each semester through the events feed announcements. Starting a new club needs a faculty advisor and a proposal to student affairs.

Club funds are managed through the student activities account; spending needs the advisor's counter-approval.

Room bookings for club meets follow the normal facilities booking process with the club's faculty advisor as the requester.
