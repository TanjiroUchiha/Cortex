# Visitor host responsibilities

## Summary
**What should the reader know?** The host remains responsible for the visitor's purpose, check-in and escort conditions; visitor access does not confer access to restricted areas.

## Action
Use the Campus Services Portal for a case-specific answer. The service desk at campus@cdu.example can route a request but cannot approve an exception outside its delegated role.

This guidance applies to campus-wide services; emergency instructions and dated unit notices take precedence.
