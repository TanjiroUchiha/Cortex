# Transport and bus routes

The institute runs buses on published routes covering major residential areas; the route list, stops and morning/evening timings are on the transport page of the portal.

A bus pass is required to board — apply on the portal under transport with your route and stop; passes are issued for the academic year and checked by the driver.

Route changes, new stops and temporary diversions are announced on the transport page and the notice boards. Requests for a new route need a minimum number of interested students and are reviewed each semester.

Report a bus breakdown, missed pickup or driver issue to the transport office with the route number and time — the office coordinates with the bus contractor.
