# Library

The central library is open 8am to 10pm on working days and 9am to 6pm on weekends and
holidays. Opening hours are extended during the examination season — current timings are
posted on the library page of the portal.

Books are borrowed on the organisation ID card for two weeks; late returns carry the fine
listed in the library rules. Reading rooms do not require booking. The borrowing counter,
digital section and reference desk keep separate timings, also listed on the portal.

Report lost books, damaged copies or issues with the reading rooms at the borrowing counter.
