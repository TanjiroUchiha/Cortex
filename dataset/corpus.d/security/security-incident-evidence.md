# Security incident evidence preservation

## Purpose and scope
This record applies to campus safety operations; immediate danger should be reported through the emergency channel. Investigators record original file location, time and handler; do not alter devices or delete messages unless the response lead directs it.

## Responsibilities
The requester supplies complete, accurate information. Campus Security verifies the record, records its decision and communicates any required next step through the approved portal.

## Process and records
Investigators record original file location, time and handler; do not alter devices or delete messages unless the response lead directs it. The responsible owner is Campus Security. Submit a case through the Security Operations Desk with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.
Keep only the records required for the case. Do not place identity documents, medical details, credentials or payment secrets in a general-purpose ticket.

## Exceptions and escalation
An exception must be approved by the named record owner and recorded with its reason and expiry. If the issue creates immediate risk, contact Campus Security; routine questions should remain with the responsible service desk.

## Review
The department owner reviews this guidance when the governing schedule, service workflow or institutional approval changes. A newer active version takes precedence over a superseded copy.

## Example
If a request related to security incident evidence preservation is incomplete, the service team returns it with the missing item and keeps the original case reference. The requester adds the evidence to that case rather than opening a second record.
