# Hostel and mess fees

Hostel rent and mess charges are invoiced per semester on the fees portal under separate heads from tuition — check that both heads are cleared if you live in a hostel.

Mess fee rebates for approved absence (internships, medical leave of more than ten days) are applied after the hostel office verifies the dates; apply on the fees portal with the approval letter attached.

Hostel fee refunds on mid-semester withdrawal follow the slab in the hostel rules — the first month is non-refundable, and later withdrawal refunds a prorated amount minus the caution deduction if any.

Changing from single to shared occupancy mid-term adjusts the hostel head on the next invoice.
