# Duplicate fee receipt request

## Summary
**What should the reader know?** Students download a duplicate receipt from transaction history or request assisted retrieval with invoice ID; receipts are reissued, not altered.

## Action
Use the Fees Portal for a case-specific answer. The service desk at fees@cdu.example can route a request but cannot approve an exception outside its delegated role.

This record applies to institutional invoices and posted transactions; it does not replace a dated invoice or circular.
