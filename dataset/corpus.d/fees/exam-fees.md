# Examination fees

Examination registration fees are paid on the fees portal under the examination head, separate from tuition. The registration window closes on the academic calendar date — late registration carries the condonation fine listed in the fee circular.

Backlog and improvement exams have per-course fees shown at registration. Hall tickets are released only after the examination fee is paid and no pending dues exist.

Fee concessions for examination fees are not available unless specifically notified by the scholarship cell.

If the portal does not show the examination head during the registration window, contact the fees counter before the deadline — a missed deadline cannot be overridden by the fees office.
