# Scholarships and refunds

Merit and need-based scholarships are applied as adjustments on the next fee invoice after approval — the scholarship office confirms the amount, the fees office posts it.

Refund requests (excess payment, hostel withdrawal deposit, caution deposit on leaving) are filed on the fees portal under refunds and processed within ten working days to the registered bank account. Caution deposit refunds additionally require a no-dues clearance from the library, labs and hostel.

Government and external scholarships routed through the institute are disbursed after verification by the scholarship cell; status is tracked on the portal rather than the fees desk.

If an approved scholarship does not appear on the next invoice, open a fees ticket quoting the scholarship approval number.

Scholarship adjustments appear on the next invoice. Refund requests are processed through the fees portal within ten working days.
