# Education Loan Letters

Education loan and bonafide fee letters

Banks and scholarship agencies regularly need an official fee-structure or bonafide-cum-fee letter for education loans. Apply on the fees portal under certificates — select the letter type and enter the bank or agency name exactly as they need it printed.

Standard letters are issued within three working days and are collected from the Student Accounts Office or downloaded as a digitally signed PDF. There is no charge for the first two copies in an academic year.

For urgent bank deadlines or a letter that must name a specific officer, write to the Student Accounts Office — fees@campus.edu (ext 4500) — with the bank's deadline.
