# Fee clearance for graduation

## Summary
**What should the reader know?** Graduation clearance requires a zero balance or an approved documented adjustment; Student Accounts publishes unresolved invoice items to the student privately.

## Action
Use the Fees Portal for a case-specific answer. The service desk at fees@cdu.example can route a request but cannot approve an exception outside its delegated role.
