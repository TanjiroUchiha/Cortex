# Tuition installment request

## Purpose and scope
This record applies to institutional invoices and posted transactions; it does not replace a dated invoice or circular. Eligible students request installments before the invoice due date; approval fixes the dates and amounts shown in the portal and does not waive late charges.

## Responsibilities
The requester supplies complete, accurate information. Student Accounts verifies the record, records its decision and communicates any required next step through the approved portal.

## Process and records
Eligible students request installments before the invoice due date; approval fixes the dates and amounts shown in the portal and does not waive late charges. The responsible owner is Student Accounts. Submit a case through the Fees Portal with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.
Keep only the records required for the case. Do not place identity documents, medical details, credentials or payment secrets in a general-purpose ticket.

## Exceptions and escalation
An exception must be approved by the named record owner and recorded with its reason and expiry. If the issue creates immediate risk, contact Campus Security; routine questions should remain with the responsible service desk.

## Review
The department owner reviews this guidance when the governing schedule, service workflow or institutional approval changes. A newer active version takes precedence over a superseded copy.

## Example
If a request related to tuition installment request is incomplete, the service team returns it with the missing item and keeps the original case reference. The requester adds the evidence to that case rather than opening a second record.

## Audit trail
The owner records the decision date, approving role and any condition attached to the decision. Access to the case is limited to the roles listed in the metadata.
