# Financial delegation matrix

## Steps
1. Confirm that the request concerns financial delegation matrix and identify the responsible unit.
2. Submit the relevant record identifier and supporting evidence through the Finance Workflow.
3. Save the case reference and check the portal for a decision or a request for clarification.
4. If the record remains unresolved after the stated service window, contact finance@cdu.example with the existing reference.

## Rule
Approvals are assigned by role and cost centre; an approver cannot authorize their own claim or bypass a required second review. The responsible owner is Finance. Submit a case through the Finance Workflow with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.

## Exception
Do not create a duplicate request while an existing case is under review; urgent safety or privacy risks use the designated emergency channel.
