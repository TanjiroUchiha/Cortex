# Failed and double payments

Online payments that fail after the amount is debited are reconciled automatically — the bank reverses the amount within seven working days in most cases.

If the reversal does not arrive, raise a fees ticket with the bank transaction reference, date and amount; the fees office traces it with the payment gateway and confirms within three working days.

If the same invoice is paid twice, the extra payment appears as a credit on the portal and can be applied to the next dues or refunded to the source account on request.

Never retry a payment while a pending transaction shows on the portal — wait for the status to settle or the second payment may also debit.
