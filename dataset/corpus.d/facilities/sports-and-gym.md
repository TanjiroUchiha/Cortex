# Sports And Gym

Sports grounds, gym and recreation

The main ground, courts and the gym are open to all students and staff on the ID card. The gym runs morning and evening slots — register on the facilities portal to get a slot band; overcrowded bands are waitlisted and notified.

Team bookings for tournaments and practice go through the sports officer; a booking confirmation controls entry when slots clash. Equipment (kits, nets, boards) is issued from the sports store against the ID card and returned the same day.

Report damaged equipment, court repairs or ground issues to the Facilities Helpdesk — facilities@campus.edu (ext 4600).

The campus gym is open 6am to 9pm on working days. A valid ID card is required for entry.
