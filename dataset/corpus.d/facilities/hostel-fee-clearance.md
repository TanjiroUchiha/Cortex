# Hostel fee payment and clearance

## Purpose and scope
This record applies to assigned residents and approved visitors in residential facilities. Residents pay the invoice by its due date and resolve damage or key charges before checkout; room access may be suspended for an unresolved hold.

## Responsibilities
The requester supplies complete, accurate information. Residential Life verifies the record, records its decision and communicates any required next step through the approved portal.

## Process and records
Residents pay the invoice by its due date and resolve damage or key charges before checkout; room access may be suspended for an unresolved hold. The responsible owner is Residential Life. Submit a case through the Residential Services Portal with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.
Keep only the records required for the case. Do not place identity documents, medical details, credentials or payment secrets in a general-purpose ticket.

## Exceptions and escalation
An exception must be approved by the named record owner and recorded with its reason and expiry. If the issue creates immediate risk, contact Campus Security; routine questions should remain with the responsible service desk.
