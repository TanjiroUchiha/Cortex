# Facilities ticket priority and updates

## Summary
**What should the reader know?** Tickets include location, impact and safe access details; emergency hazards are triaged immediately while routine work receives a scheduled service window.

## Action
Use the Facilities Service Desk for a case-specific answer. The service desk at facilities@cdu.example can route a request but cannot approve an exception outside its delegated role.

This record applies to campus buildings, shared equipment and the unit responsible for maintaining them.

A room maintenance request should include the room number, issue description and urgency level. Emergency issues go to the facilities hotline.
