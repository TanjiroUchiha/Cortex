# Lost and found

Lost items found anywhere on campus are deposited at the security control room near Gate 1.

To claim an item, carry your ID card and describe the item — details, markings or the lock-screen photo for phones. Unclaimed items are held for ninety days and then disposed of per the campus policy.

Report a lost item on the portal's lost and found page with the location and time so returned items can be matched to you.

Valuables (phones, wallets, laptops) are logged separately and handed over only to the verified owner.
