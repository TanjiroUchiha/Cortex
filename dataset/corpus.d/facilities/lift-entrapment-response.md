# Lift entrapment and elevator fault

## Summary
**What should the reader know?** Use the lift alarm/intercom and provide the building and lift number; do not force doors or attempt self-rescue while the response team is dispatched.

## Action
Use the Facilities Service Desk for a case-specific answer. The service desk at facilities@cdu.example can route a request but cannot approve an exception outside its delegated role.

This record applies to campus buildings, shared equipment and the unit responsible for maintaining them.
