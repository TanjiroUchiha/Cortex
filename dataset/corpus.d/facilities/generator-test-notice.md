# Generator testing and interruption notice

## Steps
1. Confirm that the request concerns generator testing and interruption notice and identify the responsible unit.
2. Submit the relevant record identifier and supporting evidence through the Facilities Service Desk.
3. Save the case reference and check the portal for a decision or a request for clarification.
4. If the record remains unresolved after the stated service window, contact facilities@cdu.example with the existing reference.

## Rule
Planned generator tests are announced in advance; critical equipment owners register dependencies with Facilities and use approved backup procedures. The responsible owner is Facilities. Submit a case through the Facilities Service Desk with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.

## Exception
Do not create a duplicate request while an existing case is under review; urgent safety or privacy risks use the designated emergency channel.
