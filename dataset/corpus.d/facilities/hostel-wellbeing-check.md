# Hostel resident wellbeing check

## Purpose and scope
This record applies to assigned residents and approved visitors in residential facilities. A welfare check is initiated by the warden for a stated concern and logged with minimum necessary detail; emergencies go to Security.

## Responsibilities
The requester supplies complete, accurate information. Residential Life verifies the record, records its decision and communicates any required next step through the approved portal.

## Process and records
A welfare check is initiated by the warden for a stated concern and logged with minimum necessary detail; emergencies go to Security. The responsible owner is Residential Life. Submit a case through the Residential Services Portal with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.
Keep only the records required for the case. Do not place identity documents, medical details, credentials or payment secrets in a general-purpose ticket.

## Exceptions and escalation
An exception must be approved by the named record owner and recorded with its reason and expiry. If the issue creates immediate risk, contact Campus Security; routine questions should remain with the responsible service desk.
