# Room AV equipment

Projectors, screens, microphones and speakers installed in classrooms and seminar halls are
maintained by the facilities team. Report flickering projectors, dead microphones or damaged
screens with the room number through the facilities portal; urgent issues during a scheduled
event go to the facilities hotline.
