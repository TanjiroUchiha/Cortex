# Air conditioning and cooling

Air conditioners, coolers, fans and ventilation in classrooms, labs, seminar halls and offices are maintained by facilities.

If an AC is not cooling, dripping water, tripping the power, or making noise, raise a request on the facilities portal mentioning the room number and the unit (there is a sticker on every indoor unit). For seminar halls and auditoriums booked for events, report cooling issues at least a day before the event so a technician can inspect the units.

New AC installations and relocation of existing units need a department request approved by the facilities manager — individual requests for personal rooms are not accepted.

Thermostat disputes in shared spaces are handled by the block supervisor; the recommended setting is 24°C.
