# Emergency maintenance escalation

## Purpose and scope
This record applies to campus buildings, shared equipment and the unit responsible for maintaining them. A leak, structural hazard or loss of essential service is reported to Security and the Facilities emergency queue; routine tickets are not the emergency channel.

## Responsibilities
The requester supplies complete, accurate information. Facilities verifies the record, records its decision and communicates any required next step through the approved portal.

## Process and records
A leak, structural hazard or loss of essential service is reported to Security and the Facilities emergency queue; routine tickets are not the emergency channel. The responsible owner is Facilities. Submit a case through the Facilities Service Desk with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.
Keep only the records required for the case. Do not place identity documents, medical details, credentials or payment secrets in a general-purpose ticket.

## Exceptions and escalation
An exception must be approved by the named record owner and recorded with its reason and expiry. If the issue creates immediate risk, contact Campus Security; routine questions should remain with the responsible service desk.

## Review
The department owner reviews this guidance when the governing schedule, service workflow or institutional approval changes. A newer active version takes precedence over a superseded copy.

## Example
If a request related to emergency maintenance escalation is incomplete, the service team returns it with the missing item and keeps the original case reference. The requester adds the evidence to that case rather than opening a second record.
