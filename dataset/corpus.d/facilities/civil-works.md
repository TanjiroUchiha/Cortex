# Civil works and building repairs

Walls, ceilings, floors, stairs, railings, roofs and doors in all buildings are handled by the facilities civil works team.

Report cracked tiles, seepage, broken railings, damaged doors or falling plaster on the facilities portal with photos and the exact location — a safety hazard is marked urgent and cordoned off first.

Seepage and leakage complaints peak during monsoon and are scheduled by severity; classrooms and labs are prioritised over corridors.

Renovation requests from departments (new partitions, door positions, signage) need the facilities manager's approval and are scheduled during vacations.
