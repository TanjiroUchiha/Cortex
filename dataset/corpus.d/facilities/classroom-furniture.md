# Classroom and furniture repairs

Desks, chairs, benches, blackboards, whiteboards, podiums and notice boards in classrooms and labs are maintained by facilities.

Report broken furniture, wobbly desks, damaged boards or missing marker trays on the facilities portal with the room number. Broken glass or sharp damage is treated as urgent and handled same-day.

Furniture shifting between rooms needs a facilities request — departments should not move furniture themselves because the asset register tracks items by room.

Requests for extra chairs or furniture for events are linked to the room booking and must be raised at least two days before the event.
