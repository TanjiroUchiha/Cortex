# Classroom furniture repair

## Purpose and scope
This record applies to campus buildings, shared equipment and the unit responsible for maintaining them. Report damaged desks or seating with a room number and item count; remove sharp or unstable furniture from use and mark it for collection.

## Responsibilities
The requester supplies complete, accurate information. Facilities verifies the record, records its decision and communicates any required next step through the approved portal.

## Process and records
Report damaged desks or seating with a room number and item count; remove sharp or unstable furniture from use and mark it for collection. The responsible owner is Facilities. Submit a case through the Facilities Service Desk with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.
Keep only the records required for the case. Do not place identity documents, medical details, credentials or payment secrets in a general-purpose ticket.

## Exceptions and escalation
An exception must be approved by the named record owner and recorded with its reason and expiry. If the issue creates immediate risk, contact Campus Security; routine questions should remain with the responsible service desk.

The department owner reviews this guidance when the governing schedule, service workflow or institutional approval changes. A newer active version takes precedence over a superseded copy.
