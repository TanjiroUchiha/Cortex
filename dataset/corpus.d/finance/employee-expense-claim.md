# Employee expense claim

## Purpose and scope
This control applies to institutional funds and authorized approvers, not personal spending. Claims include business purpose, cost centre, dated itemized receipts and manager approval; personal purchases are excluded from reimbursement.

## Responsibilities
The requester supplies complete, accurate information. Finance verifies the record, records its decision and communicates any required next step through the approved portal.

## Process and records
Claims include business purpose, cost centre, dated itemized receipts and manager approval; personal purchases are excluded from reimbursement. The responsible owner is Finance. Submit a case through the Finance Workflow with the relevant record identifier, date and supporting evidence; keep the generated case reference until the status is closed.
Keep only the records required for the case. Do not place identity documents, medical details, credentials or payment secrets in a general-purpose ticket.

## Exceptions and escalation
An exception must be approved by the named record owner and recorded with its reason and expiry. If the issue creates immediate risk, contact Campus Security; routine questions should remain with the responsible service desk.

## Review
The department owner reviews this guidance when the governing schedule, service workflow or institutional approval changes. A newer active version takes precedence over a superseded copy.

## Example
If a request related to employee expense claim is incomplete, the service team returns it with the missing item and keeps the original case reference. The requester adds the evidence to that case rather than opening a second record.

## Audit trail
The owner records the decision date, approving role and any condition attached to the decision. Access to the case is limited to the roles listed in the metadata.
