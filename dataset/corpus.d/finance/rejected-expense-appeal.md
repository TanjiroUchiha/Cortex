# Rejected expense claim review

## Summary
**What should the reader know?** The claimant may request reconsideration with missing evidence or policy context; re-submitting the same unsupported claim does not create approval.

## Action
Use the Finance Workflow for a case-specific answer. The service desk at finance@cdu.example can route a request but cannot approve an exception outside its delegated role.

This control applies to institutional funds and authorized approvers, not personal spending.
